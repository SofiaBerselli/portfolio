1/2 Setup Guide / Process

What we evaluated

tried

Patch the existing get-started page: the problem was the IA, not the content. Adding copy would have made it more crowded, not clearer

tried

Reuse the Payments domain PM activation page (1 year old): static list of 5 PMs, no personalisation, layout constraints prevented the recommender pattern

tried

Full 4-step journey upfront (PM activation + integration verification + payment testing + MBA upsell): ideal experience: deliberately deferred steps 2 and 3 to validate the core hypothesis first

The scope decision: Build only steps 1 (payment methods) and 4 (MBA upsell) for the first experiment. Validate whether we can cross-sell MBA to single-product merchants before committing to the complete journey.

What cracked it

Treat payment method activation as a recommender, not a settings page. Surface what's relevant to where the merchant sells, pre-select it, one-click activation. Manual override stays: we're the co-pilot, not the driver. For MBA upsell: anchor on a concrete financial improvement. 365-day payouts instead of every Wednesday is specific enough for a merchant to calculate exactly what it means for their cash flow.

Setup Guide / Performance

What success looks like

Experiment 1 engineering kicked off Week 18. Too early for results, but these are the signals that will tell us if it worked.

  • MBA activation rate: Shopify NL cohort completing bank account setup in the new flow
  • Multi-product adoption: merchants activating 2 or more products in month one
  • LTMC conversion: merchants reaching 10+ payments in 30 days (they're 2× more likely to stay)
  • Payment method completion rate: on the recommender page vs the previous static list

The problem

  • No mobile-native path for bank account linking and QR code verification

MBA Onboarding / Process

Four framings for bank selection

Each approach positions the MBA opportunity differently relative to the moment merchants choose their payout bank.

tried

As one of the banks: MBA listed alongside regular bank options, too subtle, merchants miss it entirely

tried

Value-based selection: lead with the daily payout benefit before bank details. Risked confusing merchants who just wanted to link an existing account

tried

MBA upsell as a gate: dedicated page before bank linking. Felt interruptive, merchants had a clear intent to set up payouts

tried

Contextual upsell woven in: surface the MBA benefit at the moment of bank choice, without blocking the primary task

What worked: Contextual upsell. Merchants are choosing their payout bank at exactly the moment when a better payout option is most relevant.

What cracked it

The bank selection page is the moment where the product either earns trust or loses it. Surfacing the MBA offer at the exact point where merchants are already thinking about payouts made the upsell feel like a natural next step, not a distraction. Contextual framing converted better than a dedicated upsell page because it matched what the merchant was already trying to do.

My role: Defined onboarding journeys for legal representatives and app-based identity verification across BVs, VOFs, and bank selection. Cross-functional alignment across Product, Risk, Compliance, and Ops.
