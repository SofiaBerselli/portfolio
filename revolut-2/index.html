What cracked it

The setup guide page is a living hub that promotes multiple products and lets merchants enable them. It shows only the actions relevant to merchants onboarding right now and supports self-serve activation. It also clarifies where merchants are in the journey, what's next, and how much remains to complete it.

MBA Onboarding / Process

Four framings for bank selection

Each approach positions the MBA opportunity differently relative to the moment merchants choose their payout bank.

tried

As one of the banks: MBA listed alongside regular bank options, too subtle, merchants miss it entirely

tried

Value-based selection: lead with the daily payout benefit before bank details. Risked confusing merchants who just wanted to link an existing account

tried

MBA upsell as a gate: dedicated page before bank linking. Felt interruptive, merchants had a clear intent to set up payouts

tried

Contextual upsell woven in: surface the MBA benefit at the moment of bank choice, without blocking the primary task

What worked: Contextual upsell. Merchants are choosing their payout bank at exactly the moment when a better payout option is most relevant.

What cracked it

The bank selection page is the moment where the product either earns trust or loses it. Surfacing the MBA offer at the exact point where merchants are already thinking about payouts made the upsell feel like a natural next step, not a distraction. Contextual framing converted better than a dedicated upsell page because it matched what the merchant was already trying to do.

My role: Defined onboarding journeys for legal representatives and app-based identity verification across BVs, VOFs, and bank selection. Cross-functional alignment across Product, Risk, Compliance, and Ops.
